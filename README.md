# HyderBite - Local Food Discovery App

## 📱 Project Overview

HyderBite is a comprehensive UI/UX design case study for a mobile 
app that helps users discover authentic Hyderabad cuisine, explore 
local food hotspots, and find hidden culinary gems based on their 
real-time location.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Users struggle to discover authentic local food experiences in 
Hyderabad without proper guidance. Current solutions lack:
- Authentic local cuisine information
- Real restaurant details & timings
- User-generated food photos
- Hidden gem recommendations
- Hyderabad-specific food culture

## ✨ Solution

HyderBite provides a seamless mobile app with:
- **Location-based food discovery** - Find nearby authentic restaurants
- **Authentic food focus** - Filter by Hyderabad specialties (biryani, haleem, dosa)
- **Real photos & reviews** - User-generated content, not stock images
- **Meal timing info** - Know when food is available
- **Instant booking & delivery** - Reserve tables or order food

## 🎨 Design System

- **Primary Color:** #FF6B35 (Warm orange - appetite color)
- **Secondary Color:** #F7931E (Golden/saffron - cultural)
- **Typography:** Poppins (all weights)
- **Card Radius:** 12px
- **Layout:** Grid-based, spacious design
- **Components:** 10 screens, 30+ UI components
- **Accent Colors:** Light backgrounds #FFF5E6

## 📲 10 Mobile Screens

1. **Onboarding** - Hyderabad food culture & benefits
2. **Login/Sign Up** - User authentication
3. **Home/Explore** - Nearby restaurants map view
4. **Food Discovery** - Browse by dish (biryani, haleem, etc.)
5. **Restaurant Detail** - Menu, reviews, photos, timing
6. **Food Categories** - Filter by cuisine, meal type, price
7. **Search & Filters** - Distance, rating, specialty filters
8. **Booking/Reservations** - Table booking, delivery orders
9. **Reviews & Photos** - User-generated content showcase
10. **User Profile** - Favorites, order history, preferences

## 👥 Target Users

- **Food Enthusiasts (25-40)** - Seek authentic local cuisine
- **Tourists/Travelers (28-45)** - Want genuine Hyderabad food experiences
- **Busy Professionals (22-35)** - Need quick local food discoveries nearby
- **Foodies (All ages)** - Love exploring hidden culinary gems & street food

## 🎯 Project Goals

✓ Help users discover authentic Hyderabad cuisine
✓ Showcase local food hotspots
✓ Create community of food enthusiasts
